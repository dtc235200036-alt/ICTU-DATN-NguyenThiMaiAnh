Facts fixed at planning time:
- Parallel-safety: runs in-process against a temporary SQLite thesis_ai; no docker, no MySQL, no backend import. Writes only ai-service/** and docs/chuong-4-trien-khai/thu-nghiem-ai/03-hieu-chinh-nguong.md.
- Extraction parity: calibration/extract.py copies the Web API's extract_text logic (backend/app/services/documents.py) exactly — DOCX "\n".join(p.text for p in Document(BytesIO(b)).paragraphs), PDF "\n".join(page.get_text() for page in fitz.open(stream=b, filetype="pdf")), then NFC and NUL removal — with a comment naming the source function; if they ever diverge, production numbers stop matching this report.
- Local id mapping inside the temporary index: corpus titles are ARCHIVE ids 1..300 in CSV order (manifest topics[].stt), A documents are DOCUMENT ids 1..25 (pair order), B documents are only queried (ref_id 0), never ingested — mirrors production where a submission is checked before it is ingested.
- Labels: a pair is "flagged" when do_an_trung[A] ≥ nguong_tai_lieu (the share of B attributed to its source thesis A); ty_le_tong (share matched to anything in the corpus) is reported alongside. A B sentence is positive-verbatim when it lies in a paragraph with provenance kieu NGUYEN_VAN, positive-semantic when kieu DIEN_DAT, negative otherwise; sentences are mapped to paragraphs by normalized containment in the manifest paragraph texts.
- Methods (04-RESEARCH "Don't Hand-Roll"): sklearn.metrics.roc_curve + Youden's J (sklearn is already a transitive dependency of sentence-transformers); when a signal separates the tuning split perfectly, use the midpoint between max negative and min positive for margin, and say which method fired. Thresholds are expressed in percent with one decimal.
- ROADMAP note: "Nếu hết T8 mà hiệu chỉnh chưa đạt: ghi nhận giới hạn vào báo cáo" — the soft gate records limitations; never refit on KIEM_CHUNG.
- Memory: load the chosen model once per command; no second model.
